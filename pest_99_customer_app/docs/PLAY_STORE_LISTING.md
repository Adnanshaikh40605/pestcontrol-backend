## Short description (≤80 chars)

## Full description

Pest Control 99 helps customers book professional pest control services for homes and commercial properties across our service cities.

**What you can do**
- Browse CRM rate-card prices by property size and service
- Book one-time or AMC packages
- Track bookings, AMC visits, payments & invoices
- Raise a complaint or re-service request
- Sign in securely with mobile OTP

**Payment**
Pay after service. Online gateway checkout will be enabled in a future update.

**Support**
Multi Pest Care LLP · https://www.pestcontrol99.com/contact/
