CRM mein 2 types ke technicians honge: Salaried Technician aur Partner Technician (40:60 Model).
Salaried Technician: Monthly salary, attendance, leave, incentive, monthly settlement.
Partner Technician: 40% Technician, 60% Company.
Company chemicals, pest control kit aur safety kit provide karegi.
Travelling expense technician ka hoga.
Partner technician se refundable security deposit liya jayega.
Settlement weekly ya monthly hoga.

5. Booking Types
One-Time: Partner technician ko 40% sharing milegi.
AMC: Total amount ko total services se divide kiya jayega. Har completed visit ke baad us visit value ka 40% actual service karne wale technician ko milega.
Example: AMC ₹2200 / 3 services = ₹733.33 per visit. Technician share = ₹293.33 per completed visit.
Contractual: Society, Hotel, Hospital, School, Warehouse, Factory, Commercial contracts mein percentage sharing nahi hogi. Fixed payout per visit/per job hoga.
6. Payment Model
Booking create karte waqt select hoga: Revenue Sharing / Fixed Payout / Salaried Technician.
